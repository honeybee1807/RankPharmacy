CHRONIC MEDICATION

CCMDD &
Chronic Care

If you're on chronic medication, you shouldn't have to queue at a clinic every month. Rank Pharmacy is a registered CCMDD (Central Chronic Medicine Dispensing and Distribution) pick-up point.

  • ✓Registered CCMDD collection site — collect your chronic meds here instead of the clinic
  • ✓Monthly refill reminders so you never run out
  • ✓Adherence counselling and progress check-ins
  • ✓Medication history on file — the pharmacist knows your profile
  • ✓Conditions managed: hypertension, diabetes, asthma, epilepsy, HIV, TB, mental health and more
  • ✓Private consultation room available
Ask about CCMDD enrolment

How CCMDD works

1
Doctor enrols you
Your clinic doctor registers you on the CCMDD programme at your next visit.
2
Choose Rank Pharmacy
Request Rank Pharmacy (158 Alexandra Street, Estcourt) as your preferred pick-up point.
3
We receive your script
The clinic sends your prescription directly. We prepare your medication in advance.
4
Collect monthly
Come in at your convenience — no appointment, no long queue. In and out.
