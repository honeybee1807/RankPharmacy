SERVICES

A full primary-care surface,
delivered under one roof.

Every service supervised by the owner-pharmacist, with referral paths into Estcourt's clinical network when required.

Prescription Dispensing

Acute and ongoing scripts, dispensed and counselled by a pharmacist on every interaction.

Chronic Medication (CCMDD)

Enrolled chronic patients, monthly refills, adherence support and proactive outreach.

Health Screenings

Blood pressure, glucose, cholesterol and BMI — with on-the-spot pharmacist review.

Vaccinations

Routine and seasonal immunisations — flu, travel and adult catch-up programmes.

Wellness Consultations

Private one-to-one consultations on supplements, nutrition and lifestyle.

Baby & Family Care

Infant feeding guidance, paediatric dosing and a curated baby-care category.

Wound Care

Dressings, minor wound assessment and follow-up — with referral when required.

Pharmacist Advice

Free, walk-in clinical advice from the owner — no appointment needed.
