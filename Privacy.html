Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how Rank Pharmacy collects, uses, stores and protects your personal information in compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and the SAPC Good Pharmacy Practice (GPP) patient confidentiality standards.

2. WHAT INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We only collect personal information that is necessary to respond to your enquiry or provide our pharmacy services. The information we may collect includes:

  • Contact details: Your name, telephone number and email address when you submit an enquiry via our contact form or contact us directly.
  • Health-related information: Any health or medication details you voluntarily share when submitting an enquiry. We do not collect health information unless you provide it to us for the purpose of your query.
  • Communication records: Records of communications between you and the pharmacy, including enquiry submissions, for quality and compliance purposes.

We do not collect: Payment card information, identity document numbers, or any information not necessary for your enquiry or our services. We do not use cookies for tracking purposes.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the personal information you provide solely for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry or request for information.
  • To provide pharmaceutical advice, consultation or services as requested.
  • To comply with our obligations under the Pharmacy Act 53 of 1974 and the SAPC Good Pharmacy Practice Manual.
  • To maintain records as required by applicable law and pharmacy regulations.

We will not use your personal information for marketing, share it with third parties for commercial purposes, or sell it to any party.

4. HOW WE STORE AND PROTECT YOUR INFORMATION
  • Enquiries submitted via the contact form are transmitted securely over HTTPS (SSL encrypted) and received by the pharmacy at info@rankpharmacy.co.za.
  • Email communications are stored on password-protected accounts accessible only to authorised pharmacy staff.
  • Physical patient records, where applicable, are stored securely on the pharmacy premises with restricted access as required by the SAPC GPP Manual.
  • We take reasonable and appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.
  • We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law.
5. WHO HAS ACCESS TO YOUR INFORMATION
  • Pharmacy staff: Authorised pharmacy personnel involved in responding to your enquiry or providing your care.
  • Service providers: We use EmailJS to transmit contact form submissions. EmailJS processes data in accordance with their own privacy policy available at emailjs.com. We do not share your information with any other third-party service providers.
  • Legal authorities: We may disclose your information where required to do so by law, court order, or in compliance with our obligations under the Pharmacy Act and POPIA.

We do not sell, rent, trade or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties for commercial purposes.

6. YOUR RIGHTS UNDER POPIA

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to correction: You may request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to deletion: You may request that we delete your personal information, subject to our legal and regulatory obligations to retain certain records.
  • Right to object: You may object to the processing of your personal information where we do not have a lawful basis for doing so.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: If you believe we have not handled your personal information in accordance with POPIA, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Information Regulator of South Africa:

JD House, 27 Stiemens Street, Braamfontein, Johannesburg, 2001
Email: inforeg@justice.gov.za
Website: www.inforegulator.org.za

7. HEALTH INFORMATION AND PATIENT CONFIDENTIALITY

In accordance with the SAPC Good Pharmacy Practice (GPP) Manual and the National Health Act 61 of 2003, Rank Pharmacy treats all health-related information shared with us as strictly confidential. We are bound by professional and legal obligations to protect your health information and will not disclose it to any third party without your consent, except where required by law.

If you have concerns about how your health information has been handled, you may contact the Responsible Pharmacist directly at 036 352 5201 or email info@rankpharmacy.co.za.

8. HOW TO EXERCISE YOUR RIGHTS OR MAKE A REQUEST

To exercise any of your rights under POPIA, or to make any request regarding your personal information, contact the Responsible Pharmacist:

  • In person at 158 Alexandra Street, Estcourt during trading hours
  • By telephone: 036 352 5201
  • By WhatsApp: 072 132 4385
  • By email: info@rankpharmacy.co.za

We will respond to all requests within a reasonable time and in any event within 30 days, as required by POPIA.

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
