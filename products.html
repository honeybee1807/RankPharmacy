When OTC isn't enough

Some symptoms look like minor ailments but need professional attention. The pharmacist will tell you honestly when you need a doctor — and help with a referral if required.

  • Fever above 39°C lasting more than 2 days
  • Symptoms that don't improve after 7 days
  • Chest pain, shortness of breath, or sudden severe headache
  • Blood in urine, stool or sputum

A note on supplements

Supplements can interact with prescription medications. Before starting any new supplement, especially if you're on chronic medication, please check with the pharmacist first — it's a two-minute conversation that could matter.
