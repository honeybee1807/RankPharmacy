SUPPLEMENTS

Vitamins & supplements

The supplement aisle can be overwhelming. We stock what the evidence actually supports, and the pharmacist will tell you what's worth it for your specific situation.

A note on supplements

Supplements can interact with prescription medications. Before starting any new supplement, especially if you're on chronic medication, please check with the pharmacist first, it's a two-minute conversation that could matter.
