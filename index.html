CHRONIC MEDICATION

On chronic medication?
Stop queuing at the clinic.

Rank Pharmacy is a registered CCMDD (Central Chronic Medicine Dispensing and Distribution) pick-up point. If you're on chronic medication for conditions like hypertension, diabetes, asthma or HIV — you can collect your monthly script here instead of going back to the clinic.

  • ✓No long queues — in and out in minutes
  • ✓Monthly refill reminders so you never run out
  • ✓Pharmacist adherence check-in included
  • ✓Free — your chronic script is covered
